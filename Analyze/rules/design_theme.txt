## Brand & Style
The design system is engineered for the modern real estate technology sector, focusing on clarity, trust, and
professional efficiency. The brand personality is grounded yet innovative, bridging the gap between traditional property
management and forward-thinking digital solutions.

The aesthetic follows a **Modern Minimalism** approach with a focus on high-quality typography and strategic whitespace.
It avoids the coldness of pure "tech" styles by utilizing warm neutrals and soft shapes, ensuring the UI feels
approachable for both institutional investors and individual renters. Visual noise is minimized to prioritize data
density and ease of navigation.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in a sophisticated "Teal-on-Teal" foundation to establish authority and calm.

- **Primary Teal (#266F71):** Used for primary actions, active navigation states, and brand iconography.
- **Dark Teal (#174849):** Reserved for high-contrast typography and deep-surfaces like footers or sidebars to provide a
strong visual anchor.
- **Accent Orange (#FB8E5D):** A vibrant "Action Orange" used sparingly for high-conversion CTAs, notification badges,
and interactive hover highlights.
- **Surface Palette:** Employs a crisp White (#FFFFFF) for primary work surfaces and a warm Light Gray (#F1F0EC) for
background containment and input fields to reduce eye strain.

## Typography
This design system utilizes **Manrope** for its modern, geometric construction that maintains excellent readability in
data-heavy environments. It is used for both headings and body copy to ensure a cohesive, streamlined look.

**Inter** is introduced for labels and small utility text. Its systematic nature and high x-height make it ideal for
micro-copy, data tables, and form labels where clarity is paramount.

- **Weight Usage:** Use Bold (700) for primary headlines, Semibold (600) for subheaders and navigation items, and
Regular (400) for all long-form body text.
- **Scale:** Maintain a strict 8px-based vertical rhythm in line heights to ensure a clean baseline grid.

## Layout & Spacing
The design system employs a **12-column fluid grid** for desktop and a **4-column grid** for mobile.

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 24px gutters with 40px external margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 20px gutters with 24px external margins.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 16px gutters with 16px external margins.

The spacing rhythm is strictly divisible by 8. Use `xl` (40px) for section vertical padding, and `md` (16px) for
internal card padding to maintain a sense of openness and professional breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through **Tonal Layers** supplemented by **Subtle Shadows**.

1. **Level 0 (Base):** Light Gray (#F1F0EC) background for the application canvas.
2. **Level 1 (Surface):** White (#FFFFFF) cards or containers with a 1px border (#E5E7EB) and no shadow.
3. **Level 2 (Raised):** White (#FFFFFF) surfaces with a subtle, diffused shadow (0px 4px 12px rgba(23, 72, 73, 0.05)).
Used for primary interaction cards or floating elements.
4. **Level 3 (Overlay):** Modals or dropdowns with a stronger shadow (0px 12px 24px rgba(23, 72, 73, 0.12)) to indicate
high-priority interaction.

Avoid heavy black shadows; instead, use shadows tinted with the Dark Teal (#174849) to keep the depth feeling natural
and integrated.

## Shapes
The shape language is defined by "Soft Roundedness." A standard corner radius of **16px (1rem)** is applied to primary
cards, modal containers, and large buttons. This softens the professional tone, making the software feel modern and
user-friendly.

- **Small Components:** Checkboxes and small tags use a 4px radius.
- **Standard Components:** Input fields and standard buttons use an 8px radius.
- **Feature Components:** Property cards and main containers use the full 16px radius.

## Components
- **Buttons:**
- *Primary:* Primary Teal background, White text. 16px vertical padding, 8px corner radius.
- *CTA:* Accent Orange background, White text. Used exclusively for "Book Now" or "Contact Agent" actions.
- **Input Fields:** Light Gray (#F1F0EC) fill with no border by default. Transitions to a 2px Primary Teal border on
focus.
- **Chips/Badges:** Use a soft version of the Accent Orange (10% opacity) with Dark Teal text for property status (e.g.,
"Available").
- **Cards:** White background, 16px corner radius, Level 1 or Level 2 elevation. Property images within cards should
have a matching 16px top-radius but 0px bottom-radius to sit flush against the card content.
- **Lists:** Clean rows separated by 1px dividers (#F1F0EC). Use Dark Teal for primary list text and Primary Teal for
interactive icons within the list.
- **Data Tables:** Highly legible Manrope/Inter mix. Header row should use Dark Teal background with White text for
maximum structural distinction.
