# Sales and Report Module — Technical Reference

## 1. Overview
Governs financial records, completed transactions, commission tier attribution, and historical reporting for Human Shelter Realty.

## 2. Implemented Capabilities
- Historical Closed Sales Attribution: Preserved during agent transfers or offboarding.
- Commission Tiers: Configured at the brokerage level and inherited by active agents.
- Performance Reporting: Aggregates total transaction volumes and closing velocity.
